## Accepted official evidence

### Sandero

The official grade-specific pages list the shark-fin antenna as standard for Expression and Journey. The statement is expanded to the two active Eco-G 120 configurations of each exact version.

### Sandero Stepway

The official Essential page explicitly lists `antena biczowa`; this supports a negative factory-equipment observation for the single active Essential configuration. Expression and Extreme explicitly list the shark-fin antenna as standard and are expanded only to their exact active configurations.

### Jogger

The official Essential page explicitly lists `antena biczowa`. Expression, Extreme and Journey explicitly list the shark-fin antenna as standard. The Journey page additionally lists an electric mirror with heating, sensor and electric folding; six newer standard observations correct the current state while preserving the six older negative observations as history.
