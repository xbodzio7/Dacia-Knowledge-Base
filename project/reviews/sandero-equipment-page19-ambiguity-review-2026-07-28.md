# Sandero Equipment Page 19 Ambiguity Review

## Scope

Reviewed the single ambiguous combined cruise-control and speed-limiter candidate from page 19 of the archived Sandero brochure.

## Decision

The complete row is `covered`. The brochure prints both functions as standard for Essential, Expression and Journey. The attached evidence preserves two distinct signatures, `cruise_control:standard` and `speed_limiter:standard`, with four exact Expression and Journey vehicle-sheet records. Both signatures and all records are retained without substituting one function for the other.

## Boundaries

- no `data/master` changes;
- no approved import specifications;
- no projection between trims, engines or gearboxes;
- the three brochure markers remain standard for Essential, Expression and Journey;
- the absence of attached Essential records is not filled by projecting Expression or Journey records;
- cruise control and the speed limiter remain distinct attributes.
