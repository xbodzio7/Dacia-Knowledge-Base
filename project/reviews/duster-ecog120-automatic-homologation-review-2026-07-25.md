# Duster Eco-G 120 Automatic Homologation Review — 2026-07-25

## Accepted evidence

- exact official Dacia Romania 2026 Expression, Extreme and Journey `ECO-G 120 auto, 2WD` stock pages;
- exact official Dacia Poland Expression, Extreme and Journey Eco-G 120 automatic stock pages;
- values stated directly for the automatic powertrain, including fuel-qualified engine, performance and consumption observations;
- source-stated minimum/maximum mass and payload endpoints.

## Cross-market decision

Romanian exact-stock technical evidence is accepted because each page explicitly identifies the same Duster generation, trim, Eco-G 120 powertrain, 2WD layout, automatic gearbox and model year as the registered configuration. The source market is retained as `RO` and no price, equipment availability or Romania-specific commercial assertion is imported.

The Polish source remains authoritative for the supplementary LPG WLTP CO2 value and source-stated fuel-tank capacity on the exact Polish automatic configurations.

## Accepted normalization

- power and torque are separated into `lpg` and `petrol` contexts;
- acceleration, standing kilometre and combined consumption preserve fuel context;
- engine-speed bands, empty/kerb mass and payload remain closed ranges;
- towing and authorised mass values are stored as direct scalars;
- identical values are repeated only because three independent exact trim pages prove the same automatic configuration-level observations.

## Rejected inferences

- no manual-to-automatic inheritance;
- no automatic cargo volume from generic or manual technical pages;
- no petrol CO2 inference from LPG CO2 or fuel consumption;
- no payload calculation from gross and empty mass;
- no selection of a single kerb-weight or payload endpoint;
- no Romanian market price or equipment state transferred to Polish configurations.

## Result

Accepted for import:

- two registered official-web sources;
- two model, six version and six exact-configuration source relationships;
- 60 scalar values;
- 18 closed ranges;
- an expanded 29-slot automatic Duster completeness denominator.

Deferred:

- automatic-specific cargo volume;
- petrol WLTP CO2;
- any value not explicitly tied to the automatic Eco-G 120 configuration.
