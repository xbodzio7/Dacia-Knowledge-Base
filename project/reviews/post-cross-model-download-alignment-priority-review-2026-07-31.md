# Post-Cross-Model Download Alignment Priority Review

Date: 2026-07-31

## Context

The cross-model download discoverability sequence is closed. Its implementation and closure passed all workflows while preserving release, comparison and residual boundaries.

The next package therefore must create new source-backed value rather than reopen the completed usability work.

## Repository finding

`spring` is a current model in `data/master/models.csv`. The current `data/master/sources.csv` contains 30 registered sources and no Spring source.

Official Dacia Poland pages observed on 2026-07-31 expose:

- the Spring configurator with grade, powertrain, price, equipment and technical observations;
- the brochure landing page with an official Spring download entry point;
- the official stock catalogue with exact Spring MY26 cards.

These public observations are candidate evidence only. They are not canonical project sources until a separate package captures and verifies them.

## Selection

**Spring Official Source Intake Review** is selected.

The package will determine:

- the exact official document and dynamic endpoints to capture;
- stable date, identity and hash requirements;
- grade and electric-powertrain applicability boundaries;
- non-import rules for values that cannot be tied to an exact configuration;
- the smallest follow-up source-registration package.

## Non-goals

The review does not download or archive a PDF, register a source row, create a normalized snapshot, modify configurations or values, change reporting scopes, or publish a product release.

## Deferred directions

Reporting expansion remains available after a concrete gap is recorded. PDF residual work remains closed without new evidence. A new data-products release remains unjustified without a source-backed or product delta.

## Decision

`SELECT_SPRING_OFFICIAL_SOURCE_INTAKE_REVIEW`
