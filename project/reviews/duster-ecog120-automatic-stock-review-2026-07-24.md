# Duster Eco-G 120 Automatic Stock Review — 2026-07-24

## Accepted evidence

Three exact official Dacia Poland stock cards explicitly identify 2026 Duster Eco-G 120 automatic variants in Expression, Extreme and Journey.

The normalized snapshot accepts:

- the exact version, powertrain and transmission identity of all three cards;
- catalogue gross prices only where the card explicitly uses `Cena katalogowa`;
- standard power-folding mirrors for Extreme and Journey because the corresponding basic/configured-equipment lists state electric folding.

## Rejected inferences

- promotional, financing and displayed transaction prices are not catalogue prices;
- absence of a feature from a card is not `not_available`;
- the Expression mirror state is not imported because official stock-card descriptions conflict;
- no shark-fin antenna state is imported because none of the three exact cards states the factory antenna type;
- stock-card equipment is not broadened to other engines, transmissions or versions.

## Source-lifecycle boundary

Stock listings can expire or redirect. Their normalized dated snapshot remains evidence of the exact observed claims, but it does not establish that the same vehicle remains available for sale after the observation date.

## Result

Accepted for import:

- three active configurations;
- three catalogue gross prices;
- two standard folding-mirror observations;
- one model, three version and three configuration source relationships.

Deferred:

- Expression folding-mirror availability;
- all three antenna states;
- broader Duster package and equipment capture beyond these exact cards.
