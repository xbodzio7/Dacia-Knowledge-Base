# Jogger UK Catalogue Reference Matrix

Date: 2026-08-06

## Goal

Use the official Dacia UK Jogger accessory catalogue from May 2023 as a clearly separated supplementary reference to recover part numbers and six-way compatibility cells that could not yet be extracted from the much larger Polish catalogue PDF.

## Result

- 107 catalogue rows;
- 106 rows with a part number;
- 105 unique catalogue references;
- 72 references also present in the Polish accessory price list;
- 33 references present only in the GB reference matrix;
- 26 references present only in the Polish price list;
- six compatibility columns for Essential, Expression and Extreme in five- and seven-seat bodies.

## Evidence boundary

The compatibility matrix is a GB-market May 2023 reference. It is not evidence of current Polish availability. The current Polish catalogue remains the canonical target and its URL is retained in the reconciliation metadata.

No GB price is imported. No compatibility mark is transferred to Polish MY2026 grades, engines or transmissions.

## Important differences

- Complete side-step and towbar pack references differ from the component references printed in the Polish price list.
- The same roof-box references are described as 400/480 L in the GB catalogue and 330/390 L in the Polish price list.
- Rubber-mat references appear in the GB catalogue but not in the Polish 2022 price list.
- Radio model names differ despite matching references.
- The Sleep Pack is still TBA as one complete pack, while the box, mattress, tent and blinds have separate references.

## Next step

Obtain a directly extractable Polish Jogger catalogue matrix, verify GB-only references against current Polish sources, and retain only genuinely unresolved dynamic dependencies for the configurator fallback queue.
