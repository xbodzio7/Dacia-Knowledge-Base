# Sandero Stepway Exact Design and Factory Option Normalization

Date: 2026-08-09

## Goal

Normalize the exact current Design choices and paid factory options captured for all 15 Sandero and Sandero Stepway configurations without projecting data between grades, engines or transmissions.

## Result

- registered the immutable exact-state snapshot and linked it to all 15 configurations;
- added nine exterior-colour items and 93 exact palette-membership rows;
- added 60 current selected wheel, wheel-material, wheel-design and upholstery observations;
- added 40 exact paid-option mappings across nine configurator option codes;
- updated the current EASY package amount to 1,400 PLN while preserving the six historical 1,600 PLN observations;
- added 228 source-linked master rows in total.

## Boundaries

- unselected colours are recorded as selectable without an inferred surcharge;
- the selected default colour is recorded at zero surcharge only for its exact configuration;
- zero-cost configurator controls `SGAV02`, `SGAR03` and `TCHZ0` remain outside commercial normalization because the capture does not prove a safe canonical commercial meaning;
- historical observations remain unchanged;
- visible technical highlights are not treated as a complete technical modal, and standard-equipment highlights are not projected as a complete equipment list.

## Next package

Capture the full technical-data and standard-equipment modals for all 15 exact states, reconcile them against the remaining canonical gaps, and normalize only directly supported missing or newer observations.
